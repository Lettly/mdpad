## Emphasis

Regular text, **bold text**, *italic text*, ***bold italic***, ~~strikethrough~~, and
`inline code`. Mixing them: **bold with _italic_ inside**, and `code with **stars** in it`
stays literal.

## Links

- Inline link: [GitHub](https://github.com)
- With a title: [GitHub](https://github.com 'The place where the world builds software')
- Reference link: [the Markra repository][markra]
- Collapsed reference: [markra][]
- Autolink: <https://github.com/markrahq/markra>
- Bare URL: https://editor.markra.app
- Mail link: <hello@example.com>
- Anchor: [back to headings](#headings)

[markra]: https://github.com/markrahq/markra

## Line breaks

## Characters that need escaping

\*not italic\*, \`not code\`, \[not a link\], \_not emphasised\_, \# not a heading, and a
literal backslash: \\.

## Unicode

Accents: café, naïve, Zürich. Quotes: “curly”, ‘single’, «guillemets». Dashes: en – and em —.
Arrows: → ← ↔. Maths symbols: ≈ ≠ ≤ ≥ ∞. Emoji: 🎉 📝 ✅.

## Small text tricks

Highlighted text uses double equals: ==highlighted==.

Inline HTML works inline too: press <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>, or read H<sub>2</sub>O and
x<sup>2</sup>, or <mark>mark a phrase</mark>.
